On 26 October 2024 Better Presales with Sasha published a video interview with me, "dbt at the Snowflake Summit 2024 feat. Sean McIntyre".

The interview covered what dbt was showing at Snowflake Summit 2024, and what it means for teams building on Snowflake. A conference like the Summit packs a lot of announcements into a few days, and the useful thing afterwards is to step back and ask which of them change how a team actually works. Many teams use dbt and Snowflake together, so what one of them ships tends to land directly in the other's day-to-day work. The conversation was about connecting what was shown on the stage to the practical questions of a team that builds and runs its data on Snowflake.

The interview is on YouTube.

2024 was the busy year for me. This interview sits between the AutomateDV session in March and the Snowflake hands-on lab, which came only a few days later at the end of October.
