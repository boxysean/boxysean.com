In October 2020 I gave a talk at [Coalesce](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1e-tZWOzvUc), dbt Labs' conference, called "Delivering Data On-time, Every Day". It was about the data platform at Warby Parker, where I was working at the time.

The argument was a simple one. A data team is judged on whether the data arrives when it is promised. Good models and good dashboards do not count for much if the data behind them is late, or turns up on some days and not on others. So the platform has to be built around reliable, scheduled delivery, rather than around ad-hoc reports that someone runs when they are asked for. Delivery on time is the promise the team makes to everyone who relies on its data, and the platform is there to keep that promise every day, not just on the days when everything happens to go right.

I'm writing it up here so that the talk has a home on this site alongside the rest of my work.

This was the earliest of my public dbt talks. Everything after it builds on the same premise: data that people can count on, delivered when it was promised.
