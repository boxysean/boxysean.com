On 20 October 2026, one week after [Amsterdam]({{ site.url }}/blog/2026/10/13/congratulations-you-are-a-context-engineer-amsterdam/), I gave "Congratulations! You are a Context Engineer" for a second time, at the Vienna dbt Meetup.

The argument was the same: with AI doing more of the code, the analytics engineer's job becomes assembling the context a model needs, meaning the models, the definitions, the docs and the decisions, rather than writing every transformation by hand. What changes in a retelling is where the weight falls. The first time you give a talk, you are finding out whether the argument holds together in front of a room. The second time, with a week in between, you are no longer defending the idea itself, and you can spend the time on the part that matters to the people listening: what it means for the work they go back to afterwards, now that the context around the code counts for as much as the code.
