On 24 June 2024 I was a guest on Specific Data & Strategy, Karl Ivo Sokolov's podcast, for episode S01E14. We covered dbt Cloud, Snowflake, Databricks and Data Mesh.

Those four names are usually set up as a series of choices: which warehouse, which platform, which architecture. My argument in the conversation was that the tool choice matters less than the modelling and ownership discipline around it. A team with clear models and clear owners will do well on any of these tools. A team without that discipline will struggle on all of them, and switching platforms will not fix it. Data Mesh makes the same point from the organisational side: it is mostly about who owns which data and what they promise to the people who use it, and much less about the technology underneath.

The episode is on YouTube.

This was my first long-form conversation rather than a prepared talk. Without slides or a fixed running order, the ideas had to hold up in an open back-and-forth, which is a different kind of test from a conference slot.
