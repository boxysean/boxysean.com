On 17 April 2023 the dbt Developer Blog published my article "dbt Squared: Leveraging dbt Core and dbt Cloud together at scale".

The article is about running dbt Core and dbt Cloud side by side in one organisation. dbt Core is the open-source tool you run yourself, and dbt Cloud is the hosted product, so the two are easy to frame as an either/or choice. The article does not treat them that way. It looks at how both can be in use within the same organisation, and at when each one earns its place. The aim is for the split between them to be a deliberate decision about where each one fits, rather than something an organisation ends up with by accident.

You can find it, along with my other posts there, on my author page on the dbt Developer Blog.

This was my first written piece for the dbt Developer Blog. The blog is where the arguments get longer than a conference slot allows, with room to work through the trade-offs in full instead of summarising them on a slide.
