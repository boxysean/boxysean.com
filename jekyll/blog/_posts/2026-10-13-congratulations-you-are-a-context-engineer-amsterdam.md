On 13 October 2026 I gave a talk called "Congratulations! You are a Context Engineer" at the 17th edition of the NL dbt Meetup in Amsterdam, hosted at Eraneos.

The argument starts from a change that is already under way: AI is doing more of the code. For years, the analytics engineer's work has been writing transformations by hand, one model at a time. As more of that writing moves to an AI model, the job does not go away. It becomes assembling the context the model needs: the models, the definitions, the docs and the decisions. A model can produce a transformation, but it can only produce the right one if it has the context that tells it what right means for this team and this business. Gathering that context, keeping it current and putting it where the model can use it is the job now, rather than writing every transformation yourself.

The meetup page has the event details.

This was my first talk since the 2024 run, and the argument had moved on from data delivery, where my talks began, to AI context.
