On 30 October 2024 I ran a Snowflake Virtual Hands-On Lab called "Build, Deliver and Govern Data Products at Scale Using Data Mesh Principles with dbt". The lab is listed on Snowflake's webinars site.

A hands-on lab is a different format from a talk. It is built for doing rather than listening, so the material has to work step by step and not just make sense on a slide. This lab demonstrated building and governing data products with dbt, following data-mesh principles, hands-on. Data Mesh treats data as a product that has an owner, and the lab worked through that idea in dbt: building data products, delivering them to the people who use them, and governing them so that the lines between products stay clear as more of them are added.

Snowflake's page for the lab is linked below.

This was the only lab of my 2024: doing rather than presenting. It came four days after the Better Presales interview about Snowflake Summit, at the end of what had been a busy year for me.
