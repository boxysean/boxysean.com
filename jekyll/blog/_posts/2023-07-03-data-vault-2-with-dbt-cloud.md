On 3 July 2023 the dbt Developer Blog published my article "Data Vault 2.0 with dbt Cloud".

Data Vault 2.0 is a data modelling method built for change. It splits a warehouse into hubs, links and satellites, so that new sources and new history can be added without reworking what is already there. That makes it popular in large organisations where many systems feed one warehouse, and where the record of how data changed over time matters as much as its current state. The article covers modelling with Data Vault 2.0, and how dbt Cloud fits the pattern at scale: a method like this produces a lot of models that follow the same shapes, and the work becomes manageable when those models are built, documented and run with dbt Cloud.

The article sits on my author page on the dbt Developer Blog, next to the earlier dbt Squared piece.

This article followed the dbt Squared piece by three months, and moved from how an organisation runs dbt into how it models its data. It was also the start of the Data Vault thread that I picked up again in 2024.
