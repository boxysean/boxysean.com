The video describes a loop of three things: you, your TODO list, and your AI. At the start of the day, the AI looks back over recent conversations (email, calls, Slack) and suggests a task list, which you curate. In a task session, you name a task, the AI loads it, looks up the key context and gives suggestions, and sometimes it does the work. Adding a task can start from an email. The video also shows what a useful TODO entry holds: a title, a goal, the key context (call notes, an email, a presentation), the customer, the next step and a due date.

Why is the TODO list the interface? Because to get AI to help with anything, you have to define the task clearly. A well-kept TODO entry is exactly that definition, written down once and ready to load whenever you or the AI pick the task up.

This video is the argument from my two 2026 talks, in Amsterdam and Vienna, shown rather than told.
