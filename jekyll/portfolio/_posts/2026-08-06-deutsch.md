A personal trainer for the ÖSD A2 German exam in December. Deutsche Welt is an isometric pixel-art town, Prüfungsstadt, that you pan, zoom and click around. Each building is a topic, with its number and status on a label.

The town has three levels, A1, A2 and B1, switchable in the header; the work sits on A2. Topics are organised as vocabulary themes (Familie & Beziehungen, Wohnen, Einkaufen, Arbeit & Beruf…), grammar topics (Akkusativ, Dativ, Präteritum & Modalverben, Grammatik-Fundament…) and the exam parts: Lesen, Hören and Schreiben, plus Sprechen.

Each zone is practiced with vocabulary cards and sentences, and you rate yourself on it; the rating shows on the map label. A daily practice meter tracks a goal in minutes and a streak. There is also a conjugation drill, a "Gemischtes Training" mixed deck, and a tracker for the *Nico's Weg* A2 course.

It is a browser app built with Vite, with no backend; progress is stored locally in the browser.
